Privacy Policy

Cadenze for iPhone · Last updated 21 November 2026

In short
  • Cadenze reads your workouts, fitness readings, daily activity, body measurements and sleep from Apple Health, only on your iPhone, and keeps no copy of them.
  • It collects nothing: no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your data never reaches the developer.
  • The only data that leaves your iPhone is the map area Apple Maps needs to draw a route map, and you can turn maps off.

Who we are

Cadenze is an app by Tobias Anderberg. If you have a question about this policy, write to Tobias.Anderberg.App@gmail.com.

What Cadenze reads from Apple Health

Cadenze uses Apple's HealthKit to read data. It never writes to Apple Health or changes anything in it. You choose what it may read when it first asks, and you can change that at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Cadenze.

Depending on what you allow, Cadenze reads:

  • your workouts, with their times, distance, energy, events (such as pauses and laps) and statistics;
  • heart rate, distance, cycling cadence, step count, and running and cycling power recorded during workouts;
  • workout routes, which are location data;
  • your date of birth, to estimate your maximum heart rate;
  • your sex, to weigh your training load;
  • resting heart rate, heart rate variability and VO2 max;
  • your steps and active and resting energy per day;
  • your weight, lean body mass and body fat percentage;
  • your sleep: time in bed, time asleep and sleep stages.

How Cadenze uses it

Cadenze uses this data only to show it to you: your workouts, their graphs and heart-rate zones, your weekly and yearly stats, your fitness trends, your daily activity and body composition, and your sleep. All of it is worked out on your iPhone.

Cadenze keeps no copy of your health data. It reads Apple Health when a screen needs it and holds what it read in memory only while the app is open. If you delete a workout in the Health app, it is gone from Cadenze too.

Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with anyone and never stored in iCloud by Cadenze.

What Cadenze stores

Your settings: your own maximum heart rate if you enter one, your zone boundaries, your weekly goal and whether maps are shown. They are stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Reset settings in the app's Settings clears them, and deleting the app removes them.

To load faster, Cadenze also keeps a few results worked out from each workout that ended more than two hours ago: how long your heart rate was at each value and its highest reading, and your fastest times over the personal-best distances. Not the readings themselves, your routes or any other health data. These results are kept in the app's cache folder on your iPhone, are left out of iCloud and device backups, and are never sent anywhere. Clear cached results in Settings removes them, as do Reset settings and deleting the app, and iOS may clear them when space is short; Cadenze then works them out again from Apple Health.

Maps

When you open a workout that has a route and Show maps is on, Cadenze shows the route on Apple Maps. To draw the map, your iPhone asks Apple's map service for the map area around the route. Apple receives that area and, as with any internet request, your device's IP address. This is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

The route itself, your workout and your health data are drawn on your iPhone and never sent. With Show maps off (in the app's Settings), Cadenze draws the route as a plain line and makes no request at all.

What Cadenze does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising services, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No servers of its own: apart from Apple Maps above, Cadenze makes no network connections.

Children

Cadenze collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Not medical advice

Cadenze is for training. Its zones, maximum-heart-rate estimate and fitness readings aren't medical advice and aren't meant to diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If Cadenze ever starts handling data differently, the app's own text will say so before it happens.
