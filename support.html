Cadenze Support

Cadenze shows your workouts from Apple Health, with heart-rate zones in every view.

Get in touch

Questions, problems or ideas: write to Tobias.Anderberg.App@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you expected to see. Please don't send health data or screenshots of it unless it's needed to explain the problem.

What you need

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later, and workouts in Apple Health. An Apple Watch records heart rate, routes and the fitness readings Cadenze shows; workouts from other apps and devices show too, with whatever data they recorded.

Questions

Cadenze shows no workouts

Cadenze can only show what Apple Health lets it read. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Cadenze and turn on everything under "Allow Cadenze to read", then go back to Cadenze and pull down to refresh. If Apple Health has no workouts yet, record one with your Apple Watch or a workout app first.

Older workouts are missing

Apple Health may share only recent data with an app. In Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Cadenze, check that Cadenze may read all your recorded data, not just data from now on.

There are no heart-rate zones

Zones need your maximum heart rate. Cadenze estimates it from your age (220 − age) when your date of birth is in the Health app, or you can enter your own in Cadenze's Settings under Heart rate. The workout also needs heart-rate data, which an Apple Watch records.

How are the zones set?

Each zone starts at a percentage of your maximum heart rate: 55, 72, 82, 87 and 92 % for zones 1 to 5 by default. Change them in Settings under Zone boundaries; every view follows at once. Time below zone 1 shows as "Time outside zones".

Why is the duration shorter than in the Fitness app?

Cadenze shows active time: the workout's time without pauses, so the minutes in each zone add up to the duration shown. The total time, pauses included, is in the workout's details.

Kilometres or miles?

Cadenze follows your iPhone's region. Change it in Settings → General → Language & Region.

Maps and privacy

Routes are drawn on Apple Maps, which receives the map area around the route. The route itself never leaves your iPhone. Turn maps off in Cadenze's Settings with Show maps; the route then shows as a plain line. More in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Cadenze keeps no copy of your health data, only its settings and a cache of results worked out from finished workouts. Clear cached results, in Cadenze's Settings, removes the cache; Reset settings, at the bottom, puts the settings back to their defaults and clears the cache too; deleting the app removes both. Your workouts stay in Apple Health, where you manage them.

Is Cadenze medical advice?

No. Cadenze is for training. Its zones, heart-rate estimate and fitness readings aren't meant to diagnose or treat any condition.
